# Basketry Merit Badge

## Merit Badge Overview

Basketry is a handy skill for a Scout. A basket can be a sturdy companion on campouts, carrying clothes snugly and efficiently, holding potatoes and corn for roasting over a campfire, or carrying the day’s fishing catch back to camp for dinner. Baskets and basket-weaving projects also make great gifts for family and friends.

## Merit Badge Requirements

## The previous version of the Merit Badge requirements can be found in Scoutbook

**NOTE:**  The official merit badge pamphlets are now free and downloadable [HERE](https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/Merit_Badge_ReqandRes/Pamphlets/Basketry.pdf) or can be purchased at the [Scout Shop.](https://www.scoutshop.org/)

1. Do the following:

* (a)
  Explain to your counselor the hazards you are most likely to encounter while using basketry tools and materials, and what you should do to anticipate, help prevent, mitigate, and respond to these hazards.
  *Resources:* [Cutting Tool Safety (video)](https://youtu.be/nhB-42oMZF8)
   [Hand Tool Safety (video)](https://youtu.be/jIllqEyw3rA)
   [Health and Safety Tips for Basket Weavers (PDF)](https://sites.google.com/basketweavers.org/missouribasketweaversguild/weaving-tips/health-and-safety)
* (b)
  Discuss the prevention of and first-aid treatment for injuries, including cuts, scratches, and scrapes, that could occur while working with basketry tools and materials.
  *Resource:* [How to Treat Cuts & Scrapes | First Aid Training (video)](https://youtu.be/L77rERL64zc)

2. Do the following:

* (a)
  Show your counselor that you are able to identify the following types of baskets: plaited, coiled, ribbed, and wicker.
  *Resources:* [What Are the Four Types of Baskets? (website)](https://www.wicker-basket.co.uk/blogs/news/what-are-the-four-types-of-baskets/)
   [Historical Types of Baskets (video)](https://youtu.be/Z5PttnxrB0I)
* (b)
  Describe three different types of weaves to your counselor.
  *Resources:* [How to Start a Coiled Basket (video)](https://youtu.be/knbR3kgGssE)
   [Making a Ribbed Basket (video)](https://youtu.be/Jb0tFaZytM8)
   [Weaving a Plaited Bsket (video)](https://youtube.com/shorts/yqe6R4uWzQo)

3. Plan and weave the following projects:

* (a)
  Square basket
  *Resource:* [Bamboo Basket Tokari Weaving (video)](https://youtu.be/2H0VJtOZ-Kk)
* (b)
  Round basket
  *Resources:* [How to Make a Rustic Round Basket out of Wild Vines (video)](https://youtu.be/gKYft82Yobk)
   [Round Reed Basket (video)](https://youtu.be/jnK1kUcXqO0)
   [Cherokee Basket Weaving (video)](https://youtu.be/e-ZFtsLuiMo)
* (c)
  Campstool seat
  *Resources:* [Weaving a Camp Stool (video)](https://youtu.be/qdtWwXhm0H8)

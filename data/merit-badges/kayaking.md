# Kayaking Merit Badge

## Merit Badge Overview

Kayaking has become one of the fastest-growing paddlesports in the United States. An estimated nine million Americans enjoy this sport. The most popular style of kayaking is recreational kayaking, followed by touring/sea kayaking, and whitewater kayaking. This merit badge will introduce you to recreational kayaking and help prepare you for advanced paddlesports such as tour/sea and whitewater kayaking.

## Merit Badge Requirements

## The previous version of the Merit Badge requirements can be found in Scoutbook

**NOTE:**  The official merit badge pamphlets are now free and downloadable [HERE](https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/Merit_Badge_ReqandRes/Pamphlets/Kayaking.pdf) or can be purchased at the [Scout Shop.](https://www.scoutshop.org/)

1. Do the following:

* (a)
  Explain to your counselor the hazards you are most likely to encounter while participating in kayaking activities, including weather and water-related hazards, and what you should do to anticipate, help prevent, mitigate, and respond to these hazards.
  *Resources:* [Kayaking Tips—Cold Water and Winter Paddling (Wear, Gear, and Risks) (video)](https://youtu.be/xUUX1VI7i1I)
   [Top 5 Kayak Safety Rules for Beginners (video)](https://youtu.be/WMU4jNmlCQA)
* (b)
  Review prevention, symptoms, and first-aid treatment for the following injuries or illnesses that can occur while kayaking: blisters, cold-water shock and hypothermia, heat-related illnesses, dehydration, sunburn, sprains, and strains.
  *Resources:* [OSHA Heat Illness Prevention Campaign (video)](https://youtu.be/Kr2ouLj1oW0)
   [How To Treat a Sunburn (video)](https://youtu.be/u0sLzfq_w9s)
   [First Aid Kit for Kayaking—What's in It? (video)](https://youtu.be/iLbL7fffGAk)
   [Cold Water Shock—How To Deal With Cold Water Shock (video)](https://youtu.be/rH3gZuSr6Y8)
* (c)
  Review the Scouting America Safety Afloat policy. Explain to your counselor how this applies to kayaking.
  *Resource:* [Safety Afloat (website)](https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/safety-moments/safety-afloat/)

2. Before doing requirements 3 through 8, successfully complete the Scouting America swimmer test.
**Note:** See the *Swimming* merit badge pamphlet for details about the Scouting America swimmer test.
*Resource:* [*Swimming* Merit Badge Pamphlet (PDF)](https://filestore.scouting.org/filestore/Merit_Badge_ReqandRes/Pamphlets/Swimming.pdf)

3. Do the following:

* (a)
  Review the characteristics of life jackets most appropriate for kayaking and understand why one must always be worn while paddling. Then demonstrate how to select and fit a life jacket for kayaking.
  *Resource:* [How To Choose the Right Life Jacket (video)](https://youtu.be/4y9XPNy1L1U)
* (b)
  Review the importance of safety equipment such as a signal device, extra paddle, sponge, bilge pump, flotation bags, and throw bag.
  *Resources:* [Essential Gear That All Paddlers Need To Carry On the Water (video)](https://youtu.be/L7DkmwqfcLI)
   [Kayak Safety: How To Use a Kayak Bilge Pump (How To Get Water out of Your Kayak) (video)](https://youtu.be/-z_h6ShvsHU)
   [Must Have Paddling Safety Gear | How To Kayak or Canoe (video)](https://youtu.be/xcl4I8V2Pfg)

4. Do the following:

* (a)
  Name and point out the major parts of a kayak.
  *Resource:* [Kayak Overview (video)](https://youtu.be/JJGYUG8Bfxc)
* (b)
  Review the differences in the design between recreational, whitewater, and sea or touring kayaks. Include how length, width, stability, and rocker are involved in the design of each type.
  *Resource:* [How To Choose the Right Kayak for All Types of Flatwater Paddlers (video)](https://youtu.be/2xlCx8ckfR8)
* (c)
  Explain the care, maintenance, and storage of a kayak.
  *Resource:* [How To Clean and Maintain Your Kayak (video)](https://youtu.be/DThGAXJ8hM8)

5. Discuss the following:

* (a)
  Correct methods for the use of a kayak paddle
  *Resources:* [How To Hold a Kayak Paddle (video)](https://youtu.be/RZLtB7BmNAc)
   [Proper Technique for Paddling a Kayak (video)](https://youtu.be/v0-efrwppWQ)
* (b)
  Parts of a paddle
  *Resource:* [Kayak Paddles (website)](https://www.boat-ed.com/paddlesports/paddlesports/studyGuide/Kayak-Paddles/11109901_52043/)
* (c)
  The care and maintenance of a paddle
  *Resource:* [Kayak Paddle Maintenance (website)](https://floridasplendors.com/kayak-paddle-maintenance-essential-tips/)

6. Using a properly equipped kayak with an open cockpit, a sit-on-top, or an inflatable kayak, do the following:

* (a)
  Safely capsize and perform a wet exit.
  *Resource:* [Kayak Wet Exit—How To Get Out of Your Kayak (video)](https://youtu.be/blJFzirv3-E)
* (b)
  Reenter the kayak with assistance from a buddy boat.
  *Resources:* [How To Re-Enter a Sit Inside Kayak for Beginners (video)](https://youtu.be/O_-a5ngnRew)
   [Sea Kayak T-Rescue: Deep Water Rescue / How To Get Back in Your Kayak With Help (video)](https://youtu.be/YSRCAdCSj8Q)
   [Master the Sea Kayak Scoop Rescue for Injured Paddlers (video)](https://youtu.be/wE5y_DW2h04)
   [Heel Hook—Kayak Rescue (video)](https://youtu.be/j-zpJQeiaNc)
* (c)
  Demonstrate a kayak-over-kayak rescue.
  *Resource:* [Sea Kayak T-Rescue: Deep Water Rescue / How To Get Back in Your Kayak With Help (video)](https://youtu.be/YSRCAdCSj8Q)
* (d)
  Demonstrate the HELP position.
  *Resource:* [Help/Huddle Position (video)](https://youtu.be/Z5X1K1I8xEc)
* (e)
  Capsize the kayak, swim it and the paddle to shore, and empty water from the kayak with assistance if needed.
  *Resource:* [White Water Kayak—Swim to Shore (video)](https://youtu.be/ykvS7iHeC_g)

7. As a solo paddler, use a properly equipped kayak to demonstrate the following:

* (a)
  Forward stroke
  *Resource:* [Kayak Forward Stroke (video)](https://youtu.be/yz00070X04o)
* (b)
  Backstroke
  *Resource:* [Kayak Reverse Stroke (video)](https://youtu.be/O-feCLPf-qw)
* (c)
  Forward sweep
  *Resource:* [Kayak Sweep Strokes (video)](https://youtu.be/GWpaWh8AcZw)
* (d)
  Reverse sweep
  *Resource:* [Kayak Reverse Stroke (video)](https://youtu.be/O-feCLPf-qw)
* (e)
  Draw stroke
  *Resource:* [Kayak Draw Stroke—In Water Recovery (video)](https://youtu.be/H3G8Viju93I)
* (f)
  Stern draw
  *Resource:* [Stern Draw (video)](https://youtu.be/0bWe37-jaD8)

8. As a solo paddler, use a properly equipped kayak to demonstrate the following:

* (a)
  Paddle a straight line for 15 to 20 boat lengths using appropriate strokes while maintaining trim and balance of the kayak.
  *Resource:* [Paddling a Kayak in a Straight Line (video)](https://youtu.be/7CThukQ8b0s)
* (b)
  Spin or pivot from a stationary position 180 degrees (half circle) to the right and left within two boat lengths.
  *Resource:* [Fast Sea Kayak Turn—Turning a Sea Kayak 180 Degrees Efficiently (video)](https://youtu.be/eKGsnIat1NQ)
* (c)
  Move abeam to the right 10 feet and to the left 10 feet.
  *Resource:* [How To Turn a Kayak (video)](https://youtu.be/1j1DuKmZk1o)
* (d)
  Stop the boat in one boat length.
  *Resource:* [Paddle Strokes, the Emergency Stopping Procedure (video)](https://youtu.be/kRUpSLUFMII)
* (e)
  While maintaining forward motion, turn the kayak 90 degrees to the right and left.
* (f)
  Move the kayak backward three to four boat lengths using appropriate and effective reverse strokes.
  *Resource:* [Reverse Stroke (video)](https://youtu.be/O-feCLPf-qw)
* (g)
  Paddle the kayak in a buoyed figure 8 course around markers three to four boat lengths apart.

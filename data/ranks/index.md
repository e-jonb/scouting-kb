# Scouts BSA Ranks

_As of 2026.Q1. Listed in advancement order. Requirements sourced from official BSA PDFs._

| # | Rank | Description | File |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scout | The entry rank. Focuses on basic Scouting knowledge and the Scout Oath and Law. | [scout.md](scout.md) |
| 2 | Tenderfoot | First advancement rank. Introduces camping, first aid, and Scout skills. | [tenderfoot.md](tenderfoot.md) |
| 3 | Second Class | Builds outdoor and survival skills, including navigation and cooking. | [second-class.md](second-class.md) |
| 4 | First Class | Full Scouting skills — considered a fully capable Scout. | [first-class.md](first-class.md) |
| 5 | Star | Leadership and merit badge focus begins. Requires 6 merit badges (4 Eagle-required). | [star.md](star.md) |
| 6 | Life | Advanced leadership and service. Requires 11 merit badges (7 Eagle-required). | [life.md](life.md) |
| 7 | Eagle Scout | Highest rank. Requires 21 merit badges, demonstrated leadership, and an Eagle project. | [eagle-scout.md](eagle-scout.md) |
